About me

The architect behind the code

I'm a system architect and full-stack developer with a relentless drive to build software that is both technically sound and genuinely delightful to use. My work spans the full spectrum — from crafting pixel-perfect UIs to designing distributed backend systems that scale.

My educational journey is hands-on and continuous. I believe the best learning comes from shipping real projects, earning industry certifications, and diving deep into new domains. I hold credentials in AWS, Cisco DevNet, cybersecurity, and Python — with more in progress.

Outside of professional work, I embrace "vibe coding" — entering a creative flow state to experiment with new ideas, play with UI libraries, and remember why I fell in love with building software in the first place.

Architecture first

I design systems before writing a single line. Good architecture makes everything else easier.

Performance obsessed

Every millisecond matters. I optimize at every layer from database queries to render pipelines.

Continuous learner

Technology moves fast. I stay at the cutting edge through certifications, projects, and curiosity.

Open source advocate
